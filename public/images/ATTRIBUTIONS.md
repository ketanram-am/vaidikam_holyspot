## Sacred marks (`marks/`)

Traditional Vaishnava emblems, sourced from Wikimedia Commons rather than
hand-drawn. Both were reduced to their outline paths and set to
`currentColor`/`fill=currentColor` so the site can tint them gold; the geometry
is unchanged.

- `marks/gaudiya-tilak.svg` — “Brahma Madhya Gaudiya Tilaka”, the urdhva
  pundra worn in the Gaudiya (Brahma-Madhva-Gaudiya) line, **public domain**.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Brahma_Madhya_Gaudiya_Tilaka.svg
- `marks/sudarshana-chakra.svg` — “Chakram”, a flame-edged discus,
  **CC BY-SA 4.0** — attribution and share-alike required.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chakram.svg

**Note on the shankha.** No usable standalone conch emblem exists on Wikimedia
Commons — searches return live molluscs and Thai naval ensigns. If a shankha
mark is wanted alongside the chakra it has to come from a paid emblem set or be
commissioned; nothing free and correctly licensed was found.

**Note on ISKCON.** No ISKCON logo, seal, or branding is used anywhere on this
site. ISKCON's emblem is its own trademark. The tilak above is the traditional
religious mark, which is not anyone's property.

The original files were resized and JPEG-compressed without changing their subject matter. Existing root-level material-study images retain their original project provenance.
